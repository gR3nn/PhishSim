REQUIREMENTS AND SPECIFICATIONS

Title
PhishSafe - Phishing Awareness and Simulation Management Platform

OUTLINE
The goal of PhishSafe is to help organizations improve employee security awareness through authorized phishing simulations. The application uses one authentication system and five permission-based roles: Client, Administrator, Campaign Manager, Support Agent, and Auditor / Executive. Clients build authorized campaigns using shared templates, personal customizations, target groups, wallet credits, and service plans. Administrators or Campaign Managers review each request before delivery. After an approved simulation is sent, the platform records delivery, open, click and simulated compromise events and presents them as analytics. Support Agents answer questions through an internal conversation system, while Auditors receive read-only analytics access. Simulated pages record an event only; submitted passwords are never stored. The two protected administrator accounts are gradinarumanuel99@gmail.com and alexxperian@gmail.com.

OVERALL FUNCTIONALITY
- Register and sign in through one shared authentication page.
- Automatically display the correct workspace according to the user role.
- Add services to a shopping cart and purchase monthly plans using Stripe Checkout.
- Purchase a custom campaign for €1 per recipient or redeem wallet coupons such as FREE100.
- Create and manage authorized employee audience groups with a validated semicolon-based recipient editor.
- Start with 12 shared email scenarios and 12 shared educational landing-page designs.
- Customize a default template privately; administrator edits affect everyone who has not made an override.
- Create reusable simulation email templates and educational landing pages.
- Submit a campaign for administrator review instead of sending immediately.
- Approve or reject campaign requests before emails can be delivered.
- Track sent, opened, clicked and simulated-compromise events in real time.
- View campaign analytics, recent activity and awareness indicators.
- Manage users and roles from the administrator workspace.
- Ask and answer questions through a role-aware support inbox.
- Record page visits and control clicks in an administrator audit trail.
- Support desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes, light/dark themes and keyboard-friendly controls.

PAGES, FUNCTIONALITY AND FEATURES
1. Login / Registration (/login)
   A common login and registration page for every user. Supabase Auth validates email/password accounts and also supports Google sign-in. The account email and stored profile decide whether the user sees client or administrator features.

2. Dashboard Overview (/dashboard)
   Shows delivery totals, open rate, click rate, risk events, an engagement chart, awareness score and recent campaigns. Administrators also see a shortcut to pending approvals.

3. New Campaign Request (/dashboard/campaigns/create)
   Lets a client choose a campaign name, email template, authorized target group and educational landing page. Submitting creates a campaign with pending_approval status. It does not send emails.

4. Campaign Details (/dashboard/campaigns/[id])
   Displays the selected campaign status, totals, target status and chronological event log. Live Supabase subscriptions update analytics when events occur.

5. Approval Queue (/dashboard/requests) - Administrator and Campaign Manager
   Lists campaign requests across all clients. The administrator reviews the content and audience, approves delivery, or rejects the request. Only approval starts the email-delivery function.

6. Plans, Wallet and Cart (/dashboard/services)
   Displays a €1-per-recipient custom campaign plus Starter, Growth and Enterprise packages. Clients add products to a cart, redeem coupons, view wallet credit and continue to hosted Stripe Checkout when external payment is required.

7. Audiences (/dashboard/groups)
   Lets clients create, view, edit and delete authorized target groups and the email recipients inside them.

8. Email Templates (/dashboard/templates)
   Lets clients build and manage reusable training email content. The {{link}} placeholder is replaced with a tracked educational link after approval.

9. Landing Pages (/dashboard/landing-pages)
   Lets clients create educational simulation pages. Public campaign links can display these pages, while submitted credentials are never saved.

10. Account Management (/dashboard/users) - Administrator and Campaign Manager
    Lists all Supabase Auth accounts and supports five roles. Administrators can change roles, suspend or restore sign-in, and delete accounts. Campaign Managers can manage roles and accounts but cannot suspend users. The two project-owner emails are protected administrators.

11. Stripe Payment Result (/dashboard/services/success)
    Confirms that Stripe Checkout returned successfully and guides the client toward creating a campaign request.

12. Support Inbox (/dashboard/support)
    Lets clients choose a suggested question or write their own. Support Agents, Campaign Managers and Administrators can view conversations and reply.

13. Activity Logs (/dashboard/logs) - Administrator and Campaign Manager
    Shows page visits and button/link clicks with the user, time, path and selected control for auditing and demonstrations.

USER TYPES
1. Client
   Can purchase services, manage only their own audiences/templates/landing pages, submit campaign requests, and view analytics for their own campaigns. A client cannot approve or send a campaign directly and cannot manage another account.

2. Administrator
   Has full access: platform-wide analytics, unrestricted campaign creation without a paid plan, approvals, shared-template governance, user roles, suspension/deletion controls, support, and activity logs. The initial administrators are Manuel Gradinaru and Alex Perian.

3. Campaign Manager
   Has the operational capabilities of an administrator, including unrestricted campaign creation without a paid plan, approvals, templates, role management and activity logs, but cannot suspend or restore users.

4. Support Agent
   Can access the overview and support inbox, read client questions and respond. This role cannot create campaigns, change templates, manage billing or alter user accounts.

5. Auditor / Executive (Read-Only)
   Designed for CISOs, HR and compliance officers. Can read organization-wide campaign analytics, campaign history and risk posture, but cannot create campaigns, edit resources or change settings.

Supabase manages the hosted PostgreSQL, Auth and Realtime server versions, and Stripe manages its hosted Checkout service. Those server versions are not stored in this repository. The exact client/library versions used to communicate with them are listed above, while package-lock.json locks all direct and transitive npm packages for repeatable installation.

RUNTIME SECURITY BASELINE
Node.js versions before 22.23.4 on the 22.x line are not permitted because of Node2Shell (CVE-2026-111497), a high-severity out-of-bounds write in the bundled Brotli decoder. The repository enforces Node.js >=22.23.4 and <23 through package.json, .nvmrc and an exact Docker base image. Next.js, React and Nodemailer are also pinned to the audited versions above. Run npm audit before each deployment; the audit recorded for this version reports zero known vulnerabilities.

DATABASE STRUCTURE
1. profiles
   id (UUID, primary key and reference to auth.users), email, full_name, avatar_url, role (client/admin/campaign_manager/support/auditor), created_at. Stores application-specific user information and permission level.

2. groups
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (owner), name, created_at. Represents an authorized campaign audience owned by a client.

3. targets
   id (UUID, primary key), group_id (foreign key), email, name. Contains the training recipients in an audience group.

4. email_templates
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (owner), name, subject, body_html, is_default, source_template_id, created_at, updated_at. Stores global defaults, personal templates and non-destructive user overrides.

5. landing_pages
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (owner), name, url_slug, html_content, is_default, source_template_id, created_at, updated_at. Stores shared and personalized educational pages.

6. campaigns
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (requesting client), title, template_id, group_id, landing_page_url, status (draft/pending_approval/sent/rejected), requested_at, approved_at, approved_by, rejection_reason, created_at. Connects campaign resources and implements the approval workflow.

7. events
   id (UUID, primary key), campaign_id (foreign key), target_email, event_type (sent/opened/clicked/compromised), user_agent, ip_address, created_at. Stores analytics without storing submitted passwords.

8. orders
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (buyer), stripe_session_id, plan, quantity, recipient_limit, amount_cents, currency, status, paid_with, created_at. Stores billing references and campaign entitlements. Card details remain in Stripe.

9. credit_transactions
   id, user_id, amount_cents, kind, reference, description, created_at. An immutable wallet ledger for coupons, purchases, refunds and administrator adjustments.

10. support_tickets and support_messages
   Store conversation ownership, subject, status, assignment, message sender, message body and timestamps.

11. activity_logs
   id, user_id, action, path, label, metadata, created_at. Stores authenticated page-view and click events for administrator audits.

SECURITY AND PERMISSIONS
Supabase Row Level Security limits clients to records they own. Administrator policies permit platform-wide review only for administrator accounts. Protected routes verify the signed-in user on the server. Campaign email delivery can run only from the administrator approval action. Stripe secrets, SMTP passwords and Supabase service credentials are stored in environment variables and are not committed to source control. The project must be used only for authorized educational simulations.
