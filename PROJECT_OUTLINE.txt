REQUIREMENTS AND SPECIFICATIONS

TITLE
PhishSafe - Phishing Awareness and Simulation Management Platform

PROJECT OUTLINE
PhishSafe is a responsive web application for authorized phishing-awareness training. An organization can prepare realistic training emails, select an approved employee audience, choose an educational landing page, and submit the campaign for review. A campaign is never delivered directly by a client: an Administrator or Campaign Manager first inspects the email, landing page, source code, links, and recipients, then approves it or returns it with written feedback. Approved emails contain campaign-specific tracking links. PhishSafe records delivery, open, click, and simulated-submission events and converts those events into analytics without storing submitted passwords.

The application has one shared authentication system and five permission-based user types: Client, Administrator, Campaign Manager, Support Agent, and Auditor / Executive. It also includes service plans, a shopping cart, Stripe-hosted checkout, a coupon-funded wallet, reusable content libraries, support conversations, account administration, and an audit trail. The protected project-owner accounts are gradinarumanuel99@gmail.com and alexxperian@gmail.com.

OVERALL FUNCTIONALITY
- Register or sign in with email/password or Google through one authentication page.
- Assign the correct dashboard and permissions from the authenticated user's role.
- Purchase a monthly plan or a one-time custom campaign priced at EUR 1 per recipient.
- Add services to a cart, redeem the FREE100 coupon once, and view wallet credit.
- Create authorized audience groups by typing, pasting, or uploading recipient data in the format email@example.com, Optional Name;.
- Use 12 managed email templates and 12 managed educational landing pages.
- Create personal templates and landing pages or customize a managed default without changing it for other users.
- Let an Administrator edit a managed default globally while retaining existing personal overrides.
- Create a campaign from an email template, audience group, and landing page.
- Validate purchased recipient capacity before accepting a client campaign request.
- Place every client campaign in a pending approval queue before delivery.
- Let reviewers switch between rendered Preview and Source code, list every href attribute, inspect recipients, approve delivery, or request changes with feedback.
- Restore the campaign allowance after rejection so the client can edit and resubmit the same campaign.
- Send approved emails through SMTP and generate production-safe tracked links.
- Record sent, opened, clicked, and simulated-submission events; submitted passwords are ignored and never stored.
- Display totals, rates, a 30-day engagement chart, awareness score, recent campaigns, campaign details, and live event updates.
- Manage accounts and roles according to role permissions.
- Exchange questions and replies in an internal support inbox.
- Record authenticated page views and control clicks in an administrative audit trail.
- Support desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts, light and dark themes, and keyboard-accessible controls.

PAGES, EXPLANATIONS, FUNCTIONALITY, AND FEATURES

PUBLIC PAGES
1. Home (/)
   Introduces PhishSafe, its controlled campaign workflow, analytics, services, and main calls to action. The public navigation links to all product-information pages and provides light/dark theme controls.

2. How It Works (/how-it-works)
   Explains the five-stage workflow: prepare, authorize, review, deliver, and learn.

3. Features (/features)
   Presents campaign building, content libraries, approval controls, analytics, support, and governance features.

4. About (/about)
   Explains the project's educational purpose and responsible-simulation approach.

5. Pricing (/pricing)
   Presents the custom EUR 1-per-recipient campaign and the Starter, Growth, and Enterprise monthly plans. Sign-in is required before purchase.

6. Trust Center (/trust)
   Describes authorization, privacy, access control, password non-retention, and review safeguards.

7. Login and Registration (/login)
   Provides the common email/password form for every role, new-account registration, Google OAuth, password recovery, and responsive error feedback. Supabase Auth manages authentication sessions.

AUTHENTICATED PAGES
8. Dashboard Overview (/dashboard)
   Displays sent, opened, clicked, and simulated-submission totals; open/click/risk rates; an interactive 30-day engagement chart; awareness score; recent campaigns; and pending-review shortcuts. Row Level Security and role policies determine whether analytics are personal or organization-wide.

9. New Campaign (/dashboard/campaigns/create)
   Lets authorized builders choose a title, email template, audience group, and landing page. Clients need an available plan/custom credit and cannot exceed its recipient limit. Administrators and Campaign Managers can create campaigns without billing restrictions.

10. Campaign Details and Editing (/dashboard/campaigns/[id], /dashboard/campaigns/[id]/edit)
    Shows status, statistics, target outcomes, and a chronological event log with live updates. Pending or rejected campaigns can be edited. A rejected campaign displays reviewer feedback and can be resubmitted after its allowance is restored.

11. Campaign Review Queue (/dashboard/requests, /dashboard/requests/[id])
    Available to Administrators and Campaign Managers. The queue lists requests and opens a complete review workspace. Reviewers can inspect requester details, all recipients, rendered email and landing-page previews, original HTML source, and an automatic list of href values. Approval begins delivery; rejection requires feedback and refunds the used allowance.

12. Billing, Wallet, and Cart (/dashboard/services)
    Available to Clients. Shows wallet balance, coupon redemption, service plans, cart contents, order history, and subscription management. Stripe hosts card checkout, while PhishSafe stores only order references and entitlement data.

13. Payment Result (/dashboard/services/success)
    Verifies a returned Stripe Checkout session or confirms a wallet purchase, records the order idempotently, and directs the client to campaign creation.

14. Audience Groups (/dashboard/groups and child create/view/edit pages)
    Creates, views, edits, and deletes user-owned authorized recipient groups. The editor validates semicolon-terminated email entries and supports text-file upload. Deleting a group also deletes its targets; groups still referenced by campaigns are protected by database relationships.

15. Email Templates (/dashboard/templates and child create/view/edit pages)
    Displays the shared email library and personal content. Users can create HTML templates, preview them, edit personal copies, and delete only personal resources. The required {{link}} placeholder becomes the tracked campaign URL after approval. Optional AI assistance can draft or revise editable HTML.

16. Landing Pages (/dashboard/landing-pages and child create/view/edit pages)
    Displays the shared educational landing-page library and personal content. Users can preview, create, edit, and delete personal pages. Editing a default creates a private override unless a manager is editing the managed global version. Optional AI assistance can draft or revise editable HTML.

17. User Accounts (/dashboard/users)
    Available to Administrators and Campaign Managers. Both can list accounts, change roles, and delete non-protected accounts. Only Administrators can suspend or restore sign-in. The two owner accounts cannot be demoted, suspended, or deleted.

18. Support Inbox (/dashboard/support)
    Clients can choose a suggested question or write a custom message and follow replies. Support Agents, Campaign Managers, and Administrators can view conversations, reply, and close tickets.

19. Activity Logs (/dashboard/logs)
    Available to Administrators and Campaign Managers. Shows up to 500 recent page-view and click records and supports filtering by action and user.

20. Account Settings (/dashboard/account)
    Shows the current name, email, role, and registration date. The user can edit the displayed name and change the account password.

TRAINING AND SYSTEM ROUTES
21. Tracked Campaign Landing (/[campaignName]/[groupName]/[slug])
    Opens the selected educational page from an approved email, identifies the campaign by its stable ID, and records a click event for the intended recipient.

22. Managed Landing Preview (/l/[slug])
    Displays a shared or personalized landing page from its URL slug.

23. Built-in Sign-in Simulation and Training Result (/sign-in-simulation, /training-result)
    Provides a controlled sign-in exercise. A submission records only the training event and redirects to an educational result; typed credentials are intentionally ignored and never stored.

24. API and Authentication Routes
    /auth/callback completes OAuth, /auth/signout ends a session, /api/checkout creates Stripe Checkout sessions, /api/webhooks/stripe verifies signed payment events, /api/track/.../pixel records opens, /api/activity records authenticated audit events, and /api/generate-content provides optional AI-assisted HTML drafting.

USER TYPES AND PERMISSIONS
1. Client
   Purchases services, redeems wallet credit, manages only owned audiences and personal resources, submits campaign requests, receives review feedback, and views analytics for owned campaigns. A Client cannot approve or directly send a campaign and cannot manage another account.

2. Administrator
   Has full platform access: organization-wide analytics, unrestricted campaign creation, campaign review and delivery, global resource management, account roles, suspension/restoration, account deletion, support, and activity logs. Protected Administrators cannot be demoted or removed.

3. Campaign Manager
   Has operational access to organization-wide analytics, unrestricted campaign creation, campaign review and delivery, shared resources, role changes, account deletion, support, and logs. This role cannot suspend or restore user sign-in and cannot modify a protected owner account.

4. Support Agent
   Uses the overview and support inbox to read and answer questions and close conversations. This role cannot create or approve campaigns, edit campaign resources, access billing, or manage users.

5. Auditor / Executive (Read-Only)
   Intended for CISOs, HR personnel, and compliance officers. Can read organization-wide campaign history, analytics, and risk posture but cannot create campaigns, edit content, approve delivery, purchase services, or change settings.

DATABASE STRUCTURE
The application uses Supabase PostgreSQL. UUID primary keys identify application records, foreign keys connect related tables, and Row Level Security applies ownership and role permissions.

1. auth.users (Supabase-managed)
   id (UUID, primary key), email, encrypted authentication information, provider metadata, confirmation/sign-in timestamps, and suspension metadata. Password hashes and OAuth identities are managed by Supabase Auth, not by application tables.

2. profiles
   id (UUID, primary key and foreign key to auth.users), email, full_name, avatar_url, role, created_at. The role constraint allows client, admin, campaign_manager, support, or auditor. A database trigger creates/updates a profile when an Auth user is created or changes email.

3. groups
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (foreign key to auth.users), name, created_at. Represents one authorized audience owned by a user.

4. targets
   id (UUID, primary key), group_id (foreign key to groups), email, name. Contains recipients for an audience. Deleting a group cascades to its targets.

5. email_templates
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (foreign key to auth.users), name, subject, body_html, is_default, source_template_id (self-referencing foreign key), created_at, updated_at. is_default identifies managed templates; source_template_id connects a user's private override to its shared original.

6. landing_pages
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (foreign key to auth.users), name, url_slug, html_content, is_default, source_template_id (self-referencing foreign key), created_at, updated_at. The user_id/url_slug pair is unique. Shared pages are publicly readable for campaign display, while writes remain permission-controlled.

7. campaigns
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (requester), title, subject, body_html, template_id (foreign key to email_templates), group_id (foreign key to groups), landing_page_url, status, requested_at, approved_at, approved_by (foreign key to auth.users), rejection_reason, created_at. Status is restricted to draft, pending_approval, sent, or rejected.

8. events
   id (UUID, primary key), campaign_id (foreign key to campaigns), target_email, event_type, user_agent, ip_address, created_at. event_type is restricted to sent, opened, clicked, or compromised (the simulated submission event). Deleting a campaign cascades to its analytics events. No password field exists.

9. orders
   id (UUID, primary key), user_id (foreign key to auth.users), stripe_session_id (unique), stripe_customer_id, plan, amount_cents, currency, status, billing_type, period_start, credit_consumed, quantity, recipient_limit, paid_with, created_at. Plans are custom, starter, growth, or enterprise; status is pending, paid, cancelled, or refunded. Card numbers are never stored.

10. credit_transactions
    id (UUID, primary key), user_id (foreign key to auth.users), amount_cents, kind, reference, description, created_at. Positive and negative immutable ledger rows calculate wallet balance. kind is coupon, purchase, refund, or adjustment. A partial unique index limits a coupon reference to one redemption per user.

11. support_tickets
    id (UUID, primary key), user_id (ticket owner), subject, status, assigned_to, created_at, updated_at. Status is open, answered, or closed.

12. support_messages
    id (UUID, primary key), ticket_id (foreign key to support_tickets), sender_id (foreign key to auth.users), body, created_at. Deleting a ticket cascades to its messages; message length is restricted to 1-4000 characters.

13. activity_logs
    id (BIGINT identity primary key), user_id (foreign key to auth.users), action, path, label, metadata (JSONB), created_at. Indexed by date and user/date for the administrator audit page.

DATABASE SECURITY AND BUSINESS RULES
- Row Level Security limits Clients to their own groups, targets, campaigns, orders, wallet records, personal resources, and support conversations.
- Administrators and Campaign Managers receive controlled cross-account policies for review and operations; Auditors receive read-only campaign/event access.
- Service-role access is restricted to trusted server code for account administration, public event recording, billing reconciliation, and other privileged tasks.
- Campaign delivery is available only through the authenticated approval action.
- Stripe webhook payloads are verified with HMAC-SHA256 and a five-minute timestamp tolerance.
- Secrets for Supabase, Stripe, SMTP, and AI services are stored in environment variables and excluded from Git and Docker build context.
- The application is intended only for authorized education and security-awareness exercises.

PRESENTATION DATA STATE
The final project database contains the protected and retained registered accounts plus the 12 managed email templates and 12 managed landing pages. Temporary campaigns, groups, recipients, analytics events, orders, wallet transactions, support messages, and activity logs used during development were removed before presentation.
